=== 10. Honest limitations ===
- Data is synthetic and observational: patterns are associations, not causes.
- problem_occurred appears strongly tied to alert count and sensor confidence, so the in-journey model partly recovers how the target was built. It detects trouble as it happens; it does not forecast it.
- Before departure the model barely beats the baseline: conditions alone do not predict problems well.
- Small groups (snow, heat alert, fog) give uncertain error rates. No passenger attributes exist, so fairness across passenger groups cannot be tested.
